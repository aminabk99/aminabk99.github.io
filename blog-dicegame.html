A one-player dice game, styled in maize and blue

May 2026 · GitHub

Kotlin · Android Studio

This is a small one-player Android app built in Kotlin, styled in University of Michigan maize and blue. Tap Roll Dice, watch your score change, and see how long you last before you hit the loss condition.

Each roll affects a running total score, odd rolls subtract and even rolls add, and a total rolls counter increments with every tap. When the loss condition is reached, the roll button disables and a message shows how many rolls it took to get there. Tap restart and everything resets to zero.

Hardest part: rendering the correct dice face for each roll value. Mapping the integer result to the right dot layout took careful view updates to make sure the UI refreshed cleanly on every tap without any flicker.

Most interesting part: the UMICH theming. Matching the exact maize and navy palette and adding the block M logo turned a generic dice roller into something with actual personality, which is a small detail but it's the difference between a tutorial project and one that feels like mine.
