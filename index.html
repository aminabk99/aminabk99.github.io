AzureAutoFix

Agentic Azure AD error resolution system. Classifies AADSTS error codes with a from-scratch PyTorch transformer and resolves admin-level issues via the MS Graph API in under 10 seconds. Deployed on Railway.

M365Mind

Ask your Microsoft 365 governance a question and get the exact policy back, cited. Hybrid retrieval (dense vectors + BM25) fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, reranked by a cross-encoder, with an optional local-LLM summary. Runs fully offline, formerly DocMind.
